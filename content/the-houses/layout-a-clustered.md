> **This layout is not agreed.** The prospective stewards drew it in October 2026 from public contour data, and after only a brief weekend visit walking the plots. Nobody else has been asked: not the company, its Community Management Panel (CMP) or its Board, and not the residents. It will be put to the company through the CMP, with the other three layouts, after more detailed discussions.

## The goal

> Put every home on the flattest, sunniest ground in the Residential Zone, close to each other and close to the shared facilities.

## Why it matters for the land

[[the-houses/index|The Houses]] explains what was decided before any layout was drawn and how the plots were placed. In short: seven homes, each unit up to 200 m² [IC], each on an unfenced Area of about one hectare with a 10-metre firebreak [MS], on sandy, rocky ground that a construction expert has advised against digging into [MS].

## The idea

All six plots sit on the flat top of the knoll between about 414 and 420 metres, with homes at least 100 metres apart. The Main Dwelling stands at the south-east tip of the Residential Zone, about 410 metres from the Shed, between the homes and the Production Zone.

## Pros

- The best ground of the four layouts. Five of the six platforms have less than a metre of rise and fall across them, and the worst has 1.4 metres [TBV]. That suits a build with little digging.
- The shortest daily trip. No home is more than about 17 minutes on foot from the Shed, and no home has more than 5 metres to climb to it [TBV].
- The shortest roads, water pipes and power runs, since the homes stand close together.
- One clear cluster, in the form the approval describes [IC].

## Cons

- Privacy. The closest two homes are about 103 metres apart, and each home's neighbours are close on several sides.
- Shelter. All six homes stand on the knoll top, the most exposed ground in the zone. Wind is not modelled, so this cost does not show in the scores.
- The north-west of the Residential Zone stays unbuilt. That may be a gain for the land.
