> **This layout is not agreed.** The prospective stewards drew it in October 2026 from public contour data, and after only a brief weekend visit walking the plots. Nobody else has been asked: not the company, its Community Management Panel (CMP) or its Board, and not the residents. It will be put to the company through the CMP, with the other three layouts, after more detailed discussions.

## The goal

> Give each home more room and privacy while keeping most homes on good ground near the shared facilities.

## Why it matters for the land

[[the-houses/index|The Houses]] explains what was decided before any layout was drawn and how the plots were placed. In short: seven homes, each unit up to 200 m² [IC], each on an unfenced Area of about one hectare with a 10-metre firebreak [MS], on sandy, rocky ground that a construction expert has advised against digging into [MS].

## The idea

Homes stand at least 150 metres apart. Four plots sit on or beside the knoll top between about 412 and 420 metres. Two sit lower down the north-west slope, at about 341 and 363 metres. The Main Dwelling stands at the north-west edge of the Production Zone, about 340 metres from the Shed.

## Pros

- More privacy than Layout A. The closest two homes are 150 metres apart [TBV].
- The two lower homes face north on gentler slopes below the knoll, with more shelter from wind than the knoll top.
- Four homes benefit from the knoll's level ground and short walk.

## Cons

- The two lower homes, B1 and B2, have the longest daily trip: about 26 to 28 minutes to the Shed, with a climb of about 56 to 78 metres on every return [TBV].
- Their platforms are steeper, at about 7 degrees, with about 2 metres of rise and fall [TBV]. A shallow build there would need some fill or a stepped floor.
- Longer roads, pipes and power runs to the two lower homes.
- Two groups of homes in place of one.
