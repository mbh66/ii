This section summarises the documents that shape life and decisions at Isikhova & Ironwood. Each page explains one document in plain language, so a reader can follow its main points without working through the legal text. The original documents are not published here.

Every summary leaves out the names of members and signatories, contact details and the financial terms of the company's agreements.

Facts carry [MS] when they come from the company's own documents and [IC] when they come from an official record. [[about|About This Site]] explains the tags.

- [[documents/shareholders-agreement|Shareholders' Agreement (2025)]]: how the company that owns the farm makes decisions and allocates homes and shares.
- [[documents/municipal-approval-2024|Municipal Approval for Additional Homes (2024)]]: the decision allowing six homes in addition to the main dwelling, with the conditions attached.
