This section summarises the documents that shape life and decisions at Isikova & Ironwood. Each page explains one document in plain language, so a reader can follow its main points without working through the legal text. The original documents are not published here.

Every summary leaves out the names of members and signatories, contact details and the financial terms of the company's agreements.

Facts carry [MS] when they come from the company's own documents and [IC] when they come from an official record. [[about|About This Site]] explains the tags.

## The documents

- [[documents/community-constitution|Community Constitution (2025)]]: the rules for living on the farm and the role of the Community Management Panel.
- [[documents/shareholders-agreement|Shareholders' Agreement (2025)]]: how the company that owns the farm makes decisions and allocates homes and shares.
- [[documents/consent-use-application-2024|The Application for Additional Homes (2024)]]: the company's application to the municipality, how it described the farm, and the rules it cited.
- [[documents/municipal-approval-2024|Municipal Approval for Additional Homes (2024)]]: the decision allowing six homes in addition to the main dwelling, with the conditions attached.
- [[documents/newsletter-march-2026|Newsletter, March 2026]]: the residents' first update for people interested in joining.
