The constitution is the rulebook for daily life at Isikhova & Ironwood Community Farm (IICF), the community on the land owned by Isikhova & Ironwood Forest Community (Pty) Ltd [MS]. The version summarised here is the 10th edition, dated 31 October 2025 [MS]. It covers community matters. Legal matters sit in the [[documents/shareholders-agreement|Shareholders' Agreement]] and the company's Memorandum of Incorporation, and where they conflict with the constitution, those legal documents win [MS].

The letters in square brackets show where each fact comes from. [MS] means it comes from the company's own document. [[about|About This Site]] explains the tags.

## In short

- The farm has room for up to seven households, each with about a hectare for its own use [MS].
- A Community Management Panel (CMP) runs daily life and advises the company's Board [MS].
- Homes stay unfenced and keep a 10-metre firebreak around them [MS].
- Hunting is banned and cats are kept indoors or contained [MS].
- Disputes go first to the people involved, then to mediation by the CMP [MS].

## Purpose and principles

The constitution states its purpose as follows [MS]:

> To live self-sufficiently in a purpose-driven community that nurtures land, people, and native wildlife – guided by sustainability, equity, and shared stewardship.

Its principles are mutual respect, environmental stewardship together with protection of biodiversity, non-discrimination and respect for human rights [MS].

## Who belongs

- **Shareholder Members** own a share in the company. Each is allocated an Area of about one hectare and builds a home there [MS]. They are automatically members of the community and can sit on the CMP [MS]. Membership ends when a shareholder sells their full shareholding, and they then leave the Area, the home and the farm [MS].
- **Resident Members** are adults who live permanently in a home on the farm without holding shares, including adults who live with a shareholder [MS].
- New members join by invitation. Shareholder Members sign both the shareholders' agreement and the constitution [MS].
- **Guests** are short-term visitors. Their stay needs to be notified to or approved by the CMP, and they follow the community's rules [MS].
- **Volunteers** sign a short agreement and help with the day-to-day running of the farm [MS].
- Membership can be revoked: for Resident Members under the constitution, and for Shareholder Members under the shareholders' agreement [MS].

## Rights and conduct

Members have the right to dignity, respect, privacy, safety, freedom of belief and peaceful enjoyment of the land [MS].

Members are expected to avoid illegal activity, keep dogs under control, protect indigenous plants and animals, farm with organic methods and respect their neighbours' quiet [MS]. Hunting and free-roaming cats are not allowed [MS].

Serious misconduct, such as theft, violent crime or major harm to the environment, can lead to discipline. A Resident Member can lose the right to live on the farm, and the shareholders' agreement sets out how a shareholder can be removed [MS].

## Homes and Areas

- The Site Development Plan allows up to seven homes, each on its own Area [MS].
- Each shareholder has exclusive use of their Area and may build one home on it [MS].
- Homes are meant to be modest. A size of 120 m² is encouraged and 200 m² is the upper limit [MS].
- Maximum height is 6.5 metres and a double-storey home needs the unanimous consent of all shareholders [MS].
- Areas stay unfenced, and every home keeps a firebreak of at least 10 metres [MS].
- Before building, a member tells the CMP and gets its sign-off that the design meets the community's guidelines and the Site Development Plan. The member then builds to the approved design and lodges building plans with Hessequa Municipality [MS].

## Shared facilities

The shared facilities are the second part of the Main Dwelling (a future community centre), the workshop, the bulk water system, the communal roads and other infrastructure shown on the site map [MS]. They are maintained from company funds [MS].

Repeated misuse or neglect of a shared facility can lead to a temporary loss of access, after a fair process [MS]. The company insures the shared assets, and each shareholder insures the contents of their own home [MS].

## The Community Management Panel

- **Role.** The CMP is the community's forum for daily operations, community life and decisions that the law does not reserve for the company. It advises the Board on community matters [MS].
- **Who sits on it.** At first, the Core Members (the founding shareholders). Once all Areas are allocated, each Area may have up to two CMP members, at least one of them a Shareholder Member [MS]. Each CMP member has one vote, and the chair rotates every year [MS].
- **Portfolios.** CMP members carry portfolios such as Buildings, Energy-Water-Waste, Environment, Fire, Finance, Governance, Horticulture, Maintenance and Marketing [MS].
- **Budget.** The Finance portfolio prepares an annual budget. The CMP reviews it and the Board ratifies it [MS].
- **Meetings.** An annual general meeting is held each year with at least two weeks' notice. CMP decision meetings need at least one week's notice [MS]. A general meeting needs two-thirds of Resident Members present, with at least one from each Area. A CMP decision needs two-thirds of CMP members [MS]. The CMP keeps written records of its decisions [MS].

## Disputes and discipline

A dispute between members goes through three steps [MS]:

1. The people involved try to settle it informally, with a CMP representative.
2. If that fails, the CMP mediates within 14 days.
3. If it is still unresolved, the CMP appoints an independent facilitator.

To revoke a Resident Member's membership, the CMP runs a documented process of notice, hearing, mediation and resolution. Sanctions range from warnings to a temporary loss of access to shared facilities, up to revocation of membership [MS].

A serious breach by a shareholder is handled under the dispute and arbitration rules of the shareholders' agreement. The CMP coordinates with the Board and supports mediation outside the legal process where it can [MS].

## Community funds and new shareholders

The constitution names the sources of the community's funds. Those sources are financial terms of the company's agreements, so this site does not publish them. The funds pay for maintenance, infrastructure, firefighting equipment and agreed community projects [MS]. The CMP recommends budgets and the Board approves them within the limits set by the shareholders' agreement [MS].

Selling shares is governed by the shareholders' agreement. An incoming shareholder signs both the agreement and the constitution before the shares are registered, and the CMP gives them a short induction [MS].

## Caring for the land

- **Conservation.** The priorities are removing alien plants, planting indigenous species, using permaculture and low-tillage methods, and encouraging hedgerows and swales for biodiversity and water management [MS].
- **Fire.** Every home and every other fixed structure keeps a 10-metre firebreak. The CMP coordinates firefighting plans, community water reserves, equipment and evacuation procedures, and members must follow emergency instructions [MS]. The constitution lists membership of the SCFPA (Southern Cape Fire Protection Association) among the community's fire-safety obligations [MS].

## Quick rules for residents

Annex A gives the everyday rules in short form [MS]. Every guest reads and signs it on arrival [MS].

- **Noise.** Keep noise to a minimum between 22:00 and 07:00.
- **Shared spaces.** Clean up straight after using a shared facility, return tools cleaned to their place, and leave communal kitchens ready for the next person.
- **Neighbours.** Respect neighbours' privacy and sightlines when placing fires, braais and outdoor lights.
- **Animals.** Cats stay indoors or securely contained. Dogs are always under control and do not roam outside their owner's Area. No hunting or shooting.
- **Bookings.** Book the workshop, tools, the bakkie and (in future) the community centre through the CMP. Report anything you break, and arrange its repair or replacement.
- **Fire.** Keep the 10-metre firebreak around your home and clear dry brush before the high-risk season. Store flammable materials away from communal water sources. In a fire, raise the alarm, report to the CMP Emergency Coordinator and follow evacuation instructions.
- **Building and waste.** Tell the CMP before any building, landscaping or major earthworks, and get its sign-off. Remove building waste or keep it in the designated area. No chemical pesticides or herbicides.
- **Vehicles.** Drive slowly and give way to people on foot, cyclists and wildlife. Park only in designated areas, and never block gates or fire routes.
- **Guests.** Tell the CMP in advance about overnight guests. Guests are their host's responsibility.
- **Community time.** Residents, guests and volunteers give a minimum amount of time to community tasks, as agreed by the CMP.
- **Repairs.** Report maintenance problems to the CMP Maintenance Coordinator within 24 hours. For urgent hazards, call the emergency contacts first, then tell the CMP.
- **Disputes.** Raise an issue directly with the person first. If that does not settle it, contact the CMP mediation lead within 7 days.
- **Confidentiality.** Respect confidential information about other members and the company. Public statements about the community need the CMP's prior agreement on content and timing.
- **Sanctions.** Persistent breaches lead first to warnings, then to a temporary loss of access to shared facilities, then to the CMP's disciplinary process. For shareholders, remedies under the shareholders' agreement may follow.

## Changing the constitution

- Routine community changes are made by consensus of the CMP [MS].
- Changes that affect legal rights, such as share classes or transfer rules, are made with the Board through an amendment to the shareholders' agreement or the Memorandum of Incorporation, or a special resolution [MS].
- Adopting the constitution needs the signatures of all members [MS]. On signing, each member confirms they have read it, that their guests will sign the quick rules, and that the emergency contact sheet will be kept in plain sight at home and saved on their phone [MS].
