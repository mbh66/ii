In March 2026 the residents of Isikova & Ironwood sent their first newsletter to the people interested in joining the community [MS]. It looks back at 2025, describes daily work on the farm and sets out plans for 2027. The residents asked readers for feedback [MS].

The letters in square brackets show where each fact comes from. [MS] means it comes from the residents' own newsletter. [[about|About This Site]] explains the tags.

## Looking back at 2025

### Water

- The residents set up emergency and firefighting water through their neighbours, and rainwater harvesting from the workshop roof [MS].
- Tanks at the workshop collect rain and are also connected to a neighbour's water system about 40 metres higher up, which gives good pressure for firefighting [MS].
- Overflow from the rainwater tanks runs to the dam just above the guest cottage. The dam is part of the farm's fire management plan [MS].

### The workshop

The new workshop was completed in 2025 [MS]. Like every shared facility on the farm, private homes excepted, it is there for everyone who lives on the farm [MS]. Garages may be added on either side when residents need them [MS].

### Fire

In early September 2025 a controlled burn on a neighbouring farm got out of hand when the wind changed [MS]. Since then the residents have managed their firebreaks as a full-time task [MS]. They are also creating natural firebreaks, for example by planting aloes along key routes near the guest cottage and clearing 10 metres around the workshop [MS]. Photographs in the newsletter show the veld recovering after the fire [MS].

## Daily life on the farm

- Most daily work goes into establishing food forests and gardens, growing the flock of chickens, and setting up core systems, with water management among the most important [MS].
- A garden with drip irrigation is being established below the guest cottage [MS].
- The aloe hedges keep growing, figs are being planted alongside keurbooms, and young fruit trees stand above the dam and guest cottage [MS].
- The residents work with a small farm team [MS].
- In 2026 the residents planned to finish the projects under way and limit new large initiatives [MS].

## Plans from 2027

- The residents plan to install a solar-pumped borehole from 2027 [MS]. Water diviners on the farm and geospatial experts working remotely picked the same promising spot, close to the guest house and workshop [MS].
- A new tank farm will stand at the highest point on the farm, a few hundred metres from the borehole site [MS]. It will be connected to each home when needed, most probably in 2027 when house construction begins [MS].
- The residents hope to add garage roofs to at least one side of the workshop for individual use, which could later be enclosed as full garages [MS].

## What is still open

- The dates for the borehole and for house construction are plans stated in the newsletter [MS].
- The newsletter does not mention the conditions of the 2024 municipal approval. That approval requires the [[institutions/overberg-water|Overberg Water Board]]'s comment on the water-sharing arrangement before building plans are approved [IC].
