Every page on this site is open to correction. The site's address is shared only with people involved in the process, and every page is behind a password. Write to Michael Haupt for any corrections.

## If a page names or describes you

Tell Michael what the page gets wrong or leaves out. Your account replaces what the page says, and the page shows the date of the change. If you ask for a page about you or your organisation to come down, it comes down.

## If you have something to add

Send sources, documents and first-hand accounts. A page cites them under References, and an account from a named person is attributed to that person and tagged [MS].

## Heritage sites

Rock art, graves and other heritage sites are never described or located in these pages. The National Heritage Resources Act protects them [IC], and the W2DF register [explains the Act](https://df.valleyofgrace.co.za/rules/national-heritage-resources-act). If you know of one on the land or in the district, tell Michael privately and it stays between you.
