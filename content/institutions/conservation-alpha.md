Conservation Alpha is a conservation finance firm that designs ways to pay for measured conservation results and checks those results [IC]. It is the independent verification agent for the [[the-land/cape-water-bond|Cape Water Performance-Based Bond]] [IC]. The bond's success payments are made only after the verifier confirms the clearing [IC]. The sources read for this site record no work by Conservation Alpha near the farm [MS].

Conservation Alpha has not yet been asked to comment on this page. The page names none of its staff. The key to the letters in square brackets is on [[about|About This Site]].

## What Conservation Alpha does

Conservation Alpha takes its name from the investment term "alpha", a return above that of the market as a whole [TBV]. It describes its work as designing and planning conservation programmes, improving how they are run, measuring results, and developing outcomes-based finance for nature [TBV]. It says its outcomes frameworks and monitoring systems are "rigorous, auditable and verifiable" [TBV]. It also prepares protected area management plans and assesses management effectiveness [TBV]. It says it has helped raise more than US$15 million for conservation [TBV].

The Government Outcomes Lab at the University of Oxford lists Conservation Alpha as an intermediary on outcomes-based projects in South Africa [IC]. Its website refers to a Kenyan entity as well [TBV].

## Outcomes it has measured

| Date | Event | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| March 2022 | The World Bank issues a US$150 million, five-year Wildlife Conservation Bond, whose success payment depends on the growth of black rhino numbers in Addo Elephant National Park and Great Fish River Nature Reserve; Conservation Alpha is the calculation agent that independently works out the growth rate, and the Zoological Society of London verifies it | [IC] |
| April 2026 | The Cape Water Performance-Based Bond is listed, with Conservation Alpha as independent design and verification agent | [IC] |

A member of its staff contributed training data to a model that maps invasive trees across the Cape Floristic Region, linked to NASA's BioSCape programme [TBV].

## In this district

No work by Conservation Alpha in the Duiwenhoks catchment or the Langeberg has been found in the sources read for this site [MS]. Any later issue of a bond like the Cape Water bond would need a verifier to accept a method for measuring results. [[the-land/cape-water-bond|The Cape Water Bond and Clearing Around the Farm]] sets out which of those records exist around the farm and which are missing.
