This section describes the institutions that look after the land, water and protected areas around the farm. Each page covers what the institution does in general, then what it has done in this district in date order, and ends with what is still open. None of the institutions has yet been asked to comment on its page. The key to the letters in square brackets is on [[about|About This Site]].

Institutions are one of the forms in David Ronfeldt's [TIME framework](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/frameworks/time-framework), organised by written rules, authority and fixed settlement. The [[history/index|History]] section tells the story of the district through the other forms.

## Conservation

- [[institutions/capenature|CapeNature]]: the provincial conservation authority that manages [[the-land/boosmansbos-wilderness-area|Boosmansbos]], Grootvadersbosch and the [[the-land/wilderness-nature-reserve|Wilderness Nature Reserve]], and runs the stewardship programme for private land.
- [[institutions/grootvadersbosch-conservancy|Grootvadersbosch Conservancy]]: the conservancy of private landowners beside Grootvadersbosch and Boosmansbos.
- [[institutions/helihack-initiative|The HeliHack Initiative]]: the volunteer project that fells invasive pines from helicopters, including in Boosmansbos above the [[the-land/duiwenhoks-river-and-dam|Duiwenhoks Dam]].

## The Cape Water bond

- [[institutions/the-nature-conservancy|The Nature Conservancy]]: the conservation organisation that set up the Greater Cape Town Water Fund and carries out the clearing the bond pays for.
- [[institutions/greater-cape-town-water-fund|Greater Cape Town Water Fund]]: the partnership that clears invasive trees from the catchments that fill Cape Town's dams, and coordinates the work the bond pays for.
- [[institutions/conservation-alpha|Conservation Alpha]]: the firm that independently verifies the results on which the bond pays.

## Water

- [[institutions/department-of-water-and-sanitation|Department of Water and Sanitation]]: the national department that holds water in trust and built the Duiwenhoks Dam.
- [[institutions/breede-olifants-catchment-management-agency|Breede-Olifants Catchment Management Agency]]: the agency that manages water resources in the catchments that take in the Duiwenhoks.
- [[institutions/overberg-water|Overberg Water]]: the water board that runs the Duivenhoks scheme below the dam.
- [[institutions/duiwenhoks-irrigation-board|The Duiwenhoks Irrigation Board]]: the farmers' board that shares the dam's water for irrigation.

## Local government

- [[institutions/hessequa-municipality|Hessequa Municipality]]: the local municipality for Heidelberg and the farm, which supplies water to the town and is the planning authority for the farm.

## How pages are written

Each page describes an institution only through what it has said or done, and never says what it needs, wants or would accept. Where an institution's work touches the company that owns the farm, the page records the official decision and says nothing more until the company has replied. When an institution already has a page on the [Ward 2 Development Forum register](https://df.valleyofgrace.co.za), the page here covers only what bears on this land and links to that entry.
