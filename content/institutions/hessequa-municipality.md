Hessequa Municipality is the local municipality for Heidelberg and the farm [IC]. It lies in the Garden Route District, and its seat is Riversdale [TBV]. It supplies water to Heidelberg, Slangrivier and Witsand, buying bulk water from [[institutions/overberg-water|Overberg Water]]'s Duivenhoks scheme [IC]. It is also the planning authority whose letter gave notice of the 2024 decision on additional homes on the farm [IC].

The municipality has not yet been asked to comment on this page. The key to the letters in square brackets is on [[about|About This Site]].

## What the municipality is

The municipality was created at the local elections of December 2000 as the Langeberg Municipality [TBV]. It was renamed Hessequa in June 2005 [TBV]. Its towns include Riversdale, Heidelberg, Slangrivier, Albertinia, Stilbaai, Witsand, Vermaaklikheid and Gouritsmond [TBV]. It covers 5,733 square kilometres, and the 2022 census counted 71,918 people living there [TBV].

The name comes from the Hessequa, the Khoekhoe people who grazed cattle and sheep between Swellendam and Albertinia before 1652 [IC]. The Ward 2 Development Forum register holds the history of the [Hessequa](https://df.valleyofgrace.co.za/history/tribes/hessequa).

## Water

Hessequa Municipality takes nearly 2 million litres a day from Overberg Water's Duivenhoks scheme for its towns [IC]. In February 2018, after the dam had filled, the municipality kept water restrictions in place across the region [TBV]. It met the provincial department of local government, the Eden District Municipality and the Breede-Gouritz Catchment Management Agency that month to assess the drought [TBV].
