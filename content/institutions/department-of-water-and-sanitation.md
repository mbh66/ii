The Department of Water and Sanitation is the national government department responsible for South Africa's water resources [IC]. Under the National Water Act of 1998, the national government holds the country's water as public trustee [IC]. Near the farm, the department's predecessor built the [[the-land/duiwenhoks-river-and-dam|Duiwenhoks Dam]] in the early 1960s [TBV]. The department reports the dam's level each week [IC].

The department has not yet been asked to comment on this page. The key to the letters in square brackets is on [[about|About This Site]].

## What the department does

The department began in 1912 as the Department of Irrigation of the Union of South Africa [TBV]. It later became the Department of Water Affairs and Forestry [TBV]. In May 2009 forestry moved to another department, and in May 2014 the Department of Water and Sanitation was established [TBV].

The National Water Act of 1998 sets the rules the department administers. It places all water in national trust and gives first priority to the Reserve: the water needed for basic human needs and to keep rivers, wetlands and estuaries healthy [IC]. The Ward 2 Development Forum register explains the Act in its entry on the [National Water Act](https://df.valleyofgrace.co.za/rules/national-water-act).

The Act lets the Minister set up catchment management agencies to manage water in each water management area, and water user associations for local users [IC]. The Minister is also the executive authority over the water boards, among them [[institutions/overberg-water|Overberg Water]] [IC]. The [[institutions/breede-olifants-catchment-management-agency|Breede-Olifants Catchment Management Agency]] manages water resources in the area that takes in the Breede and Gouritz rivers [IC].

## In this district

| Date | Event | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Early 1960s | The national water department builds the Duiwenhoks Dam | [TBV] |
| 1972 to 1984 | The department gauges the river at a station named "Duiwenhoks River at Broken Hill", about 2 km south-west of the farm | [IC] |
| 2014 | Reserve determination studies for the Gouritz water management area, including an intermediate assessment of the Duiwenhoks estuary | [IC] |
| 2016 | The country's water management areas are redrawn as nine areas; the Duiwenhoks falls in the Breede-Gouritz area | [TBV] |
| 26 August 2022 | The Minister publishes Reserve determinations for water resources of the Breede-Gouritz water management area, in Government Gazette 46798 | [IC] |
| 2022 | Government Gazette 47559 extends the Breede-Gouritz Catchment Management Agency into the Breede-Olifants Catchment Management Agency | [IC] |
| November 2022 | Notice of the Minister's intention to extend Overberg Water's service area to the whole Western Cape | [IC] |

The estuary study of 2014 lists a representative of the then Breede-Overberg Catchment Management Agency among the people consulted [IC]. Its findings could not be read for this site.
