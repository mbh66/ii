Farmers below the [[the-land/duiwenhoks-river-and-dam|Duiwenhoks Dam]] share its water through an irrigation board [TBV]. A local newspaper named it the Duivenhoks-besproeiingsraad in 2018 [TBV]. Its water bailiff divides the supply between grazing and vegetable farmers, sharing it as fairly as possible [TBV]. Little else about the board has been found in published sources [MS].

The board has not yet been asked to comment on this page. The page names no members or officers. The key to the letters in square brackets is on [[about|About This Site]].

## What irrigation boards are

Irrigation boards were bodies of landowners that ran shared irrigation works and divided water among their members under the water laws before 1998 [TBV]. The National Water Act of 1998 provides for them to become water user associations [TBV]. The Minister establishes a water user association by notice, as with the Buffelsjagsriver Water User Association in the Tradouw and Buffeljags catchments in 2023 [IC].

The family's research file gives an annual allocation of about 3.7 million cubic metres for irrigation from the dam [TBV]. The [[history/overview|historical overview]] sets out the allocations it records. Overberg Water's 2021/22 annual report estimates that the old irrigation canal from the dam loses 30 to 50 percent of its flow [IC].
