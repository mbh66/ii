The Breede-Olifants Catchment Management Agency is the body set up under the National Water Act to manage water resources in the Breede-Olifants water management area [IC]. That area joins the former Breede-Gouritz and Berg-Olifants areas, and its four large rivers are the Breede, the Berg, the Gouritz and the Olifants [IC]. The Duiwenhoks lies between the Breede and the Gouritz, in the former Breede-Gouritz area [TBV].

The agency has not yet been asked to comment on this page. The key to the letters in square brackets is on [[about|About This Site]].

## What a catchment management agency does

The National Water Act provides for catchment management agencies to manage water resources at catchment level [IC]. The agency's general functions include advising on water resource management, developing a catchment management strategy, coordinating water-related work in its area and promoting community participation [IC]. The Breede-Gouritz agency described its aims as water resources planning, water use management, water allocation reform and water resource protection, among others [IC]. In 2025 the Breede-Olifants agency named alien vegetation removal, water quality and sub-catchment strategies among its priorities [IC].

## How the agency grew

| Date | Event | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| By 2013 | The Breede-Overberg Catchment Management Agency, known as BOCMA, is operating | [TBV] |
| 2014 | The agency's area is extended and it becomes the Breede-Gouritz Catchment Management Agency | [TBV] |
| 2016 | The country's water management areas are redrawn as nine areas | [TBV] |
| 2022 | Government Gazette 47559, Notice 2795, extends the Breede-Gouritz agency's boundary and area of operation to establish the Breede-Olifants Catchment Management Agency | [IC] |
| 2024 | The Department of Water and Sanitation reduces the planned number of agencies to six, the Breede-Olifants agency among them | [IC] |
| 2025 | The agency reports more than 75 staff, with costs raised by the merger and the staff it absorbed | [IC] |

## In this district

The Department of Water and Sanitation's 2014 study of the Duiwenhoks estuary lists a representative of the Breede-Overberg agency among those consulted [IC]. In February 2018, during the drought, the Breede-Gouritz agency met Hessequa Municipality, the provincial department of local government and the Eden District Municipality to assess the drought and possible measures [TBV].
