The Grootvadersbosch Conservancy is an association of farmers and landowners in the Grootvadersbosch valley north-west of Heidelberg, organised as a non-profit trust [TBV]. It borders [[the-land/grootvadersbosch-nature-reserve|Grootvadersbosch Nature Reserve]] and the [[the-land/boosmansbos-wilderness-area|Boosmansbos Wilderness Area]] [TBV]. Its members farm the valley and promote conservation on their own land [TBV].

Most of what this page reports comes from the conservancy's own listing, so it carries [TBV] until a second source confirms it. The conservancy has not yet been asked to comment on this page. The page names no members. The key to the letters in square brackets is on [[about|About This Site]].

## What a conservancy is

A conservancy is a voluntary arrangement among neighbouring landowners to look after the natural features of their land together [TBV]. CapeNature counts conservancies among the conservation areas in its Biodiversity Stewardship programme, the most flexible of its four options, with no fixed commitment period [IC].

## What the conservancy says it does

| Item | What the conservancy says | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Founded | 1992, when local farmers and land users joined forces; it describes itself as the first conservancy in the Western Cape | [TBV] |
| Area | About 35,000 hectares, across the Overberg and Garden Route districts | [TBV] |
| Members | 19 landowners, most of them long-established commercial farmers | [TBV] |
| Stewardship | Three registered stewardship sites | [TBV] |
| Help for members | Help paying for alien plant clearing, fire planning and joint firefighting, shared security cameras and tourism promotion | [TBV] |
| Natural features | Fynbos, Afromontane forest and critically endangered renosterveld; the Cape ghost frog and the Grootvadersbosch dwarf chameleon, found only in this valley | [TBV] |
| Trails | Mountain bike and hiking trails across members' land, with a short section inside the nature reserve | [IC] |

CapeNature confirms that a short section of the conservancy's cycle trail lies inside Grootvadersbosch Nature Reserve, and that riders need a permit from the conservancy office [IC].
