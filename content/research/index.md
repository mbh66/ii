This section holds research briefs. A brief gathers what published work says about a question the land raises, and sets out how far that evidence can support a proposal. Proposals in [[proposals/index|Proposals]] cite the brief they rest on, so a reader can check the evidence behind an idea before judging the idea itself.

## How a brief is laid out

- An opening paragraph that says what the brief covers and what it found.
- A table of what the evidence can and cannot carry for each kind of proposal.
- The evidence itself, with a tag after every finding.
- The limits of the search, and what is still open.
- References, one line per source, saying what the brief took from it.

A brief records evidence and proposes nothing. Where a brief draws on a longer review held on another site, it links to the full review and keeps on this site only what bears on the land.
