A proposal is an idea for the land that nobody has agreed to yet. Each proposal gets one page. The page opens with a notice that says who has been asked, who has not, and when the idea will be put to the people who would carry it out.

## How a proposal is laid out

- The goal, in one sentence.
- Why the land needs it, in tagged facts.
- The idea, what it would deliver, who would benefit, who would pay and who would decide.
- What is still open, including each named party's own view.
- What happens next, and how to have your say.
