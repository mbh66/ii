This section tells the history of the land and the district around it. Pages are grouped by three of the four forms in David Ronfeldt's [TIME framework](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/frameworks/time-framework).

## Start here

- [[history/overview|The Duiwenhoks Valley: A Historical Overview]]: the river valley from Khoekhoe grazing land to the dam, with a timeline, a tenure table and the questions still open.

## How pages are grouped

- **Tribes.** One page for each people whose story touches the land, organised by kinship and belonging.
- **Institutions.** One page for each institution that shaped the district, organised by written rules, authority and fixed settlement.
- **Markets.** One page for each way the district exchanged goods, labour, money and land, organised by exchange at a price.

## How pages are written

Where the written record was made by outsiders, a page says so, and it sets out each account where sources disagree. Names that many people find offensive appear only where a page explains them or quotes a source. Descendants of the peoples described may live in the district today, and their own accounts correct any page. Pages about peoples and institutions that still exist carry a note on whether they have replied.

When a people or an institution already has a page on the [Ward 2 Development Forum register](https://df.valleyofgrace.co.za), the page here covers only what bears on this land and links to that entry.

Rock art, graves and other heritage sites are never located on this site. [[how-to-respond|How to Respond]] explains how to share such a site privately.
