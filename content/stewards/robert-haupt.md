Robert Haupt, born in 1973, is the youngest of the three Haupt siblings considering joining Isikhova & Ironwood as land stewards [MS]. He has always worked with his hands, and he started his first business at the age of 20 [MS]. His career has included practical trade businesses, corporate and private construction, restaurant ownership and kitchen management as a head chef [MS].

He solves problems easily, which makes getting things done quickly and efficiently come naturally to him [MS]. Running small businesses has strengthened his entrepreneurial and people skills [MS].

## Construction

After a three-year apprenticeship he opened his first business, in painting [MS]. Over 15 years it grew into a well-established construction business in the Hillcrest area of KwaZulu-Natal [MS]. During that time he also worked as a finishing foreman for Grid Construction and for Stefanutti Bressan/Stocks [MS].

He now runs a home maintenance business in Riebeek Kasteel in the Western Cape [MS].

## Restaurants

Robert joined his wife in running South Africa's first fine-dining vegan restaurant [MS]. The business later changed into a more traditional restaurant, and as head chef he was responsible for menu design and the cooking of meals, among other work [MS]. Before COVID he took over as head chef at another local restaurant [MS]. After COVID he went into partnership to open a new restaurant, which ran successfully for two years before he moved to the Cape [MS].

## The outdoors and natural health

His interest in the outdoors and natural health led him to a game ranger course at Phinda, which he did not complete, and to a diploma in herbal medicine and health [MS]. He has many years of experience growing herbs and vegetables in a private capacity [MS].

He has travelled widely in Africa, on several trips to Botswana, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Malawi and Kenya [MS]. He is studying for a Bachelor of Theology through distance learning [MS].

## Family

Robert has been married for 27 years [MS]. He and his wife have two adult children [MS].

## What he would bring to the land

Whether the family joins is the company's decision. The family's proposal will be set out under [[proposals/index|Proposals]] once it has been put to the company. If the company agrees, Robert would bring:

- Building and trade skills, from construction and carpentry to plumbing, painting, basic electrical work, general maintenance and property development [MS].
- Experience of building and maintaining properties, and of growing herbs and vegetables [MS].
- His labour, as the person who could manage the farm's building projects and other upgrades.
- The ability to get things done. He describes himself as a peacemaker who can always find the best in someone else [MS].
