Michael, Veronica and Robert Haupt are three of five children [MS]. Michael was born in 1966, Veronica in 1970 and Robert in 1973 [MS]. The family describes the three as having always stood somewhat outside the system [MS]. They are now considering joining Isikhova & Ironwood as land stewards [MS]. This page tells how their separate routes came back together on this land.

## Three routes

### Michael

Michael spent fifteen years building billing and business systems for telephone and satellite carriers in Africa, Australia, Asia, the Americas and Europe, and stepped out of that career in 2004 [MS]. He has lived in Greyton since 2013 [MS]. He convenes the Ward 2 Development Forum [IC] and records the valley's history and people on public sites [MS]. [[stewards/michael-haupt|Michael's page]] tells his route in full.

### Veronica

Veronica's work has centred on natural healing [MS]. She has built businesses of her own and worked in business rescue [MS]. [[stewards/veronica-haupt|Veronica's page]] will carry her own account.

### Robert

Robert taught himself to build in relation to the land [MS]. He has also learnt to see economic opportunity in a place, with the local community as the glue that holds it together [MS]. [[stewards/robert-haupt|Robert's page]] will carry his own account.

## Coming back to the land
