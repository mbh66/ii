Michael, Veronica and Robert Haupt are three of five children [MS]. Michael was born in 1966, Veronica in 1969 and Robert in 1973 [MS]. The family describes the three as having always stood somewhat outside the system [MS]. They are now considering joining Isikhova & Ironwood as land stewards [MS]. This page tells how their separate routes came back together on this land.

## Three routes

### Michael

Michael spent fifteen years building billing and business systems for telephone and satellite carriers in Africa, Australia, Asia, the Americas and Europe, and stepped out of that career in 2004 [MS]. He has lived in Greyton since 2013 [MS]. He convenes the Ward 2 Development Forum [IC] and records the valley's history and people on public sites [MS]. His current work shows rural communities how they can take part in performance-based nature bonds [MS], and he has written proposals with his siblings for holding water on this land, none of them yet put to the company [MS]. [[stewards/michael-haupt|Michael's page]] tells his journey in full.

### Veronica

Veronica bought her first business at 21 and spent most of her career in business, from product invention and consulting to IT sales [MS]. In 2011 she spent four years on a farm in KwaZulu-Natal, where she learnt to grow organic vegetables and came to know more than 50 healing herbs [MS]. She later trained as a psychotherapist, and after COVID she re-engineered a company that was close to bankruptcy [MS]. [[stewards/veronica-haupt|Veronica's page]] tells her journey in full.

### Robert

Robert started his first business at 20 [MS]. After an apprenticeship he grew a painting business into a construction business in KwaZulu-Natal over 15 years, and he now runs a home maintenance business in the Western Cape [MS]. He has also run restaurant kitchens as head chef, and he holds a diploma in herbal medicine and health [MS]. [[stewards/robert-haupt|Robert's page]] tells his journey in full.

## Coming back to the land

In October 2026 the siblings camped on the farm [MS] after being introduced to the farm by Veronica. The family's proposal to the company will be set out under [[proposals/index|Proposals]] once it has been put to the company.
