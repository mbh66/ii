Veronica Haupt, born in 1969, is the middle of the three Haupt siblings considering joining Isikova & Ironwood as land stewards [MS]. She bought her first business at the age of 21 and has spent most of her working life in business [MS]. In 2011 she left conventional business for four years on a farm, where she learnt to grow organic vegetables and to work with medicinal herbs [MS]. She later trained as a psychotherapist [MS]. She is now semi-retired [MS].

## A career in business

Veronica's career has spanned entrepreneurship, product invention, business consulting, business re-engineering, high-end IT sales and sales consulting [MS].

Her natural ability lies in working out what has to be done to reach a goal, and then doing it [MS]. She can pinpoint where problems are likely to arise, and she devises and carries out strategies to head them off [MS].

## Four years on a farm in KwaZulu-Natal

In 2011 she had the chance to step out of traditional business, and she spent four years on a farm in KwaZulu-Natal [MS]. There she learnt to grow organic vegetables [MS]. She also discovered the healing properties of herbs and built a working knowledge of more than 50 of them [MS].

## Psychotherapy practice

In 2015 she went back into the corporate world to do some consulting [MS]. In 2017 she left it for good [MS]. She trained as a psychotherapist and built an online coaching business focused on relationships [MS]. She ran it until the COVID-19 pandemic, when she moved to the Cape [MS].

## After COVID

From her base in Hermanus she consulted to a human development company, re-engineering its business [MS]. In two years the company went from the edge of bankruptcy to a multi-million rand business [MS]. She has since semi-retired [MS].

## Alternative healing

Her greatest personal interest is alternative healing, which she describes as healing the human body outside the mainstream medical model [MS]. She keeps a small library of books on the subject and a small stock of alternative medicines [MS].

## What she would bring to the land

Whether the family joins is the company's decision. The family's proposal will be set out under [[proposals/index|Proposals]] once it has been put to the company. If the company agrees, Veronica would bring:

- Investment in the land and its buildings.
- Experience of team and people dynamics, and the organisational skills to get things done. She describes herself as reliable and trustworthy, and as someone who brings good energy [MS].
- Her experience of growing organic vegetables, her knowledge of herbs and alternative healing, and her library and stock of alternative medicines [MS].
- Her labour, possibly as the farm's health consultant. She could share that role with Robert's wife, who also has skills and knowledge in this area [MS].
