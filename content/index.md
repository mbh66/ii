Isikhova & Ironwood is a 202-hectare parcel of land near Heidelberg, Western Cape [MS]. A family is considering joining it as land stewards [MS]. This site holds that family's working proposal for the story of the land: where it sits, what happened on it, who lives there now, and what the people who care for it could do next.

## Start here

- [[the-land/the-lands-story|The Land's Story]]: the story the prospective stewards believe the land wants to tell, and the open questions behind it.

## Sections

- [[the-land/index|The Land]]: the farm as a place, with its water, vegetation, wildlife, and fire risk.
- [[history/index|History]]: the peoples, institutions, and markets that shaped the land and the district.
- [[institutions/index|Institutions]]: the public bodies and local organisations that look after the land, water and protected areas around the farm.
- [[community/index|Community]]: the company and the people who live on the farm, in their own words.
- [[research/index|Research]]: briefs that gather the evidence behind the proposals, and say where it runs out.
- [[proposals/index|Proposals]]: ideas for the land that nobody has agreed to yet.
