Michael Haupt writes this site with his family, who are considering joining Isikhova & Ironwood as land stewards [MS]. The farm is owned by a company, and its residents live under a [[documents/community-constitution|community constitution]] [MS]. The site collects what is known about the land, its past and its present community, and sets out ideas for its future as proposals. A proposal is an idea. It becomes a decision when the people who would carry it out have agreed to it.

## How the site works

The site borrows its practices from the [Ward 2 Development Forum register](https://df.valleyofgrace.co.za) in the Valley of Grace, Overberg District, where Michael and his daughter have been actively involved in a similar process since 2013.

- Every factual claim carries a tag in square brackets. The key is below.
- A page about a person or an organisation says whether that party has been asked to reply.
- A proposal opens with a notice that says who has and has not been asked.
- History pages are grouped as Tribes, Institutions and Markets, three of the four forms in David Ronfeldt's [TIME framework](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/frameworks/time-framework), the grouping the W2DF register uses for its [Valley History](https://df.valleyofgrace.co.za/history/).
- Individual residents are not named. Contact details, the financial terms of the company's agreements, and the location of heritage sites do not appear.

> [!info] What the letters in square brackets mean
> Facts on this site are followed by a tag that shows where they come from.
>
> - **[IC] Independently Corroborated.** Confirmed by at least two sources that do not depend on each other, or by an official record.
> - **[MS] Mission-Sourced.** Comes from the company's own documents or statements, or from the family writing this site, in their own words. It has not been checked against other sources.
> - **[TBV] To Be Verified.** Seems likely, but nobody has confirmed it yet.
>
> Statements without a tag are proposals. They describe what an idea could become.
